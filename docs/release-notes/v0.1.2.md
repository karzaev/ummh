More reliable microphone capture and better behavior with external displays.

- Uses input-only AUHAL capture adapted from VoiceInk, including USB microphones and closed-lid MacBook fallback.
- Opens the recording panel on the display under the cursor and keeps it there until it closes. If the display disconnects, the panel moves to an available display.
- Keeps the display awake during recording and reduces menu-bar updates while showing live text.

Verified with 52 automated tests and live dictation using system-default and USB
display microphones with the lid open and closed.

**Apple Silicon · macOS 26+**

Open the DMG and drag ummh into Applications. This build is ad-hoc signed and
not notarized by Apple. macOS may require first-launch approval.
[Apple's guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/102445).

Models download on first use. Recognition runs locally with Parakeet TDT v3.
Microphone and Accessibility access are required.
